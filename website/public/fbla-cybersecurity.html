Practice (answers below)

1. A compliant laptop requests payroll access. Give a Zero Trust decision.

Verify user, device, and resource-specific policy; allow only the required payroll action for an appropriate session. LAN/VPN presence alone is not authorization.

2. Convert binary 1101 to decimal and one hex digit.

8+4+1=13 decimal; four bits map to hex D. Hex is a representation of the same bits, not encryption.

Practice (answers below)

1. Why do device attestation and identity proofing establish different facts?

Attestation lets a requester validate signed hardware measurements; proofing links a human claim to a real person. Keys, measurement trust, and proofing evidence each have limits.

2. A finance analyst needs one report. Name a least-privilege grant.

Grant read access only to that report/view, log its use, and review or revoke when duties change; broad database administration is unnecessary risk.

Practice (answers below)

1. Which password minimum applies: single factor or MFA-only?

NIST SP 800-63B-4 sets 15 characters for single-factor use and 8 when used only as part of MFA. Identify the context before selecting the minimum; do not apply one value to both.

2. A tester sees an admin host outside written scope. What next?

Stop activity on that host and notify the named contact under the rules of engagement. Authorization is limited to the documented scope; curiosity does not extend permission.
