Practice (answers below)

1. A PC has a valid LAN address but cannot reach an off-subnet server. Which SOHO settings and devices would you check first, and why?

Check mask, DHCP-provided default gateway, router WAN/ISP handoff, then name resolution separately. The router forwards off-subnet traffic; DNS only resolves names. Integrated gateways may combine modem, router and AP roles.

2. Why can a permit ACL above a deny rule change results, and when is /31 appropriate?

Cisco IOS ACLs evaluate in order to first match, then implicit deny applies if none match. /31 uses its two addresses as hosts only on a point-to-point link whose endpoints support it, not a normal shared LAN.

Practice (answers below)

1. An office has 40 users, two printers, and rotating staff. Compare a host-shared printer with a print server.

Host sharing is simple but jobs depend on that host's uptime and controls. A print server can centrally manage queues and permissions, but introduces a service to maintain and secure; choose based on support and availability needs.

2. Why does a blade chassis improve density but not automatically improve resilience?

Blades share chassis infrastructure such as power, cooling and interconnects, increasing density and management convenience while creating common failure dependencies. Validate independent feeds and capacity; redundancy labels alone do not prove independence.

Practice (answers below)

1. A compliant school laptop connects to a guest VLAN but cannot reach an internal file server. Explain a Zero Trust/NAC design reason.

NAC may admit the device only to a restricted segment; authorization can still deny the file-server resource. Verify identity, posture and the specific permitted flow rather than assuming LAN presence grants trust.

2. Contrast BitLocker, AD DS, and SELinux; which one is a volume-encryption feature?

BitLocker encrypts Windows volumes to reduce offline exposure; AD DS centrally stores identities and administers authentication/access; SELinux applies Linux MAC policy to process/object interactions. None alone replaces layered controls.

Practice (answers below)

1. A ransomware scenario has a recent encrypted NAS copy and a week-old offline copy. Which restore choice should the team investigate?

Investigate the offline version as a potentially cleaner restore point, while validating age against the RPO and checking integrity in an isolated environment. NAS proximity alone does not make it safe; RAID and online copies may share compromise.

2. An IPS blocked a legitimate file-transfer flow. What differs from IDS behavior, and what should operators do?

IDS primarily detects and alerts; IPS can enforce a block inline, so a false positive can interrupt service. Review evidence and rule scope, safely tune with change control, then validate both desired and prohibited flows.

1. A small office wants shared files without a server. What should it weigh?
