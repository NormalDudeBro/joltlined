Practice (answers below)

1. Buyer wants a colorful dashboard. What question tests the need?

Ask what task it supports, who uses it, and what outcome matters. A want may be valuable, but confirm its purpose rather than relabel it as a need.

2. Why identify user, payer, and approver separately?

They may have different requirements and authority. Map roles before asserting fit; one person's preference is not automatically the group's need.

1. What belongs in a consent-based follow-up agreement?

Purpose, preferred channel, timing, responsible owner, and promised action. Keep only necessary details, honor opt-out, and reset expectations if delayed.

2. A judge interrupts during a recommendation. How does the speaker resume clearly?

Answer the question fully and accurately, then use a concise transition to the next buyer-centered point. FBLA has no separate Q&A period.
