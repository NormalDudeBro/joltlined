o Overview
-> Deliver clearly and responsively
-> Answer questions completely
o Deliver clearly and responsively
-> Poise is attentive, prepared recovery, not forceful bravado.
-> Use natural eye contact; avoid staring or looking away.
-> Projection means clear audible speech, not shouting.
-> Pace and pauses make important distinctions easier to follow.
-> Open posture and purposeful gestures support spoken meaning.
-> Practice aloud; familiarity helps manage nerves and fluency.
-> Rehearsal supports fluency and audience-focused attention.
-> Pause briefly after a stumble, then recover the thread.
-> Delivery skill never substitutes for truthful content or fit.
o Answer questions completely
-> Listen to the whole question before answering.
-> Clarify ambiguous scope rather than guessing.
-> Restate the issue briefly and answer every part.
-> Use verified product scope, evidence, and limitations.
-> Say honestly when a fact is unknown.
-> Agree on a realistic follow-up date only with permission.
-> Do not improvise specifications or guarantees.
-> Separate confirmed facts from estimates in every answer.
-> Engage the judge while answering, not with a canned dodge.
-> Transition back and check if the answer resolved the question.
o Overview
-> Know the spoken rating sheet
-> Follow event rules and ownership
o Know the spoken rating sheet
-> Current rubric scores ten spoken criteria separately.
-> Each spoken criterion bands: 0, 1-6, 7-8, or 9-10.
-> Protocol criterion scores 0 or 1-10, not binary 0/10.
-> Maximum: 10 x 10 + 10 protocol = 110 points.
-> Protocol zero means guidelines were not followed.
-> Protocol performance includes permitted devices and conduct.
-> Questions are part of interactive delivery, not extra Q&A.
-> Use this current rubric, not obsolete all-binary guidance.
-> Judge scoring is distinct from event penalties.
o Follow event rules and ownership
-> Preliminary: one or two laptop-sized devices maximum.
-> If two devices, one faces judges and one presenters.
-> Preliminary: no power, Internet, projectors or speakers.
-> Use the presenting device for preliminary audio.
-> No judge interaction during setup; remove materials afterward.
-> Links/QR may display, but judges may not click or scan.
-> Only approved service animals; no musical instruments.
-> Food may be displayed, not served or left with judges.
-> Document credible research and copyrighted assets used.
-> Only competitors prepare and set up their own entry.
