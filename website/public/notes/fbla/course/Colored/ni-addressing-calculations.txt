(pen)o (li)Subnet purpose and CIDR(pen)

(pen)-> (li)CIDR(pen) uses explicit prefixes, not inferred (p)address(pen) (p)classes(pen).
(pen)-> (li)Subnet(pen) design balances growth, (p)address(pen) use and broadcasts.
