(pen)o (pu)Social Engineering(pen)
(pen)-> Phishing deceives users into revealing data or unsafe action.
(pen)-> Spear-phishing tailors lures to a person or group.

(pen)o (db)DoS and DDoS Mechanics(pen)

(pen)-> SYN flood leaves TCP handshakes incomplete, holding state.

(pen)o (mb)Hash Collisions and Birthday Effect(pen)
(pen)-> A (mb)hash(pen) maps input data to a fixed-length digest.
(pen)-> Collision means distinct inputs share a finite digest.
(pen)-> Preimage seeks input for a chosen digest: a different goal.
(pen)-> For ideal n-bit (mb)hash(pen), generic collision work is about 2^(n/2).
(pen)-> (mb)Birthday(pen) (mb)effect(pen) finds any pair sooner than target preimage.
(pen)-> This is a probability/work estimate, not an exact threshold.
(pen)-> Collision is not decryption or original-message recovery.
(pen)-> Hashing differs from reversible, key-based encryption.
(pen)-> RFC 6962 uses domain-separated SHA-256 Merkle-tree hashes.
