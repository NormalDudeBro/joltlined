(pen)o (p)Virus Varieties and Other Malware(pen)
(pen)-> (r)Malware(pen) is software used to harm, spy, extort, or control.
(pen)-> A (r)virus(pen) attaches to a host file or boot area to spread.
(pen)-> File (r)virus(pen) infects executable files when they run.
(pen)-> Boot-sector (r)virus(pen) targets startup records or boot media.
(pen)-> Polymorphic (r)viruses(pen) vary code/signature during replication.
(pen)-> Variation may evade simple signatures; behavior checks help.
(pen)-> (r)Trojan(pen) masquerades as benign software to induce execution.
(pen)-> A (r)worm(pen) self-propagates across systems or networks.
(pen)-> Logic bomb activates when a time/event condition is met.
(pen)-> One (r)malware(pen) sample may combine more than one behavior.
