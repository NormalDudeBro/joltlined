# Technical reference

[Back to the collection README](../README.md) · [Development guide](development.md)

Detailed content specifications, source conventions, presentation decisions, and verification coverage for maintainers. Unless a path is explicitly rooted at the repository, site-page paths (such as HTML files, `assets/`, `styles/`, and `notes/`) are relative to `website/public/`. Generator, test, and package paths (such as `scripts/`, `tests/`, and package commands) are relative to `website/`. Documentation paths are relative to the repository root. This convention preserves the detailed reference without repeating the new directory prefix in every existing path.

All thirteen site JPEGs are stored in `assets/images/`. Keep the `assets/` directory alongside the HTML files when copying an offline preview; bare image filenames below refer to files within that directory.

The collection has thirteen HTML pages: eight top-level subject-navigation destinations (including Home and the FBLA hub), plus the Algebra 2 flashcard page and four secondary FBLA event courses. Home links to seven collection destinations. The eight-link subject navigation is shared; the event courses also provide local navigation. Preview exports include all thirteen pages.

`index.html` is the offline-ready collection home, linking to **AP Lang Notes** (`ap-lang.html`), **AP World History Notes** (`ap-world.html`), **Algebra 2 Notes** (`algebra-2.html`), **FBLA Study Plan** (`fbla.html`), **Out Of My Mind Analysis** (`out-of-my-mind.html`), **Of Mice And Men Analysis** (`of-mice-and-men.html`), and **Fahrenheit 451 Analysis** (`fahrenheit-451.html`). Home uses local assets and system fonts and needs no JavaScript. The analysis pages use the same academic style as Home, with optional JavaScript navigation/accessibility controls and complete no-JavaScript documents. The FBLA page uses native answer disclosure, original plaintext notes, and explicit source-edition caveats; see the [FBLA guide](fbla.md).

World notes at `ap-world.html` cover Units 1–4: The Global Tapestry, Networks of Exchange, Land-Based Empires, and Transoceanic Interconnections, within the overall c. 1200–1750 period. Unit 3's four official topics plus two supplements total twelve pages and 288 lines. All notes are present in the HTML, without JavaScript, fetching, external fonts, or runtime dependencies. Native unit/topic anchors work offline, including `file://`. Unit 4 has a distinct palette; its sources and citation record are in the [Unit 4 source guide](unit-4-sources.md). The Unit 3 supplements retain their established coloring and original plain-text files.

The subject navigation opens **AP Lang notes** at `ap-lang.html`, titled **AP Lang Unit 1: Rhetorical Analysis**. The finished Unit 1 page preserves 96 note lines in 12 sections of eight lines each, covering rhetorical situation, Describe, Function, Effect, Justify, a worked connection, framing and diction, evidence and reasoning, credibility and emotion, objections and qualifications, relationships and sequence, and revision. Original notes in `notes/ap-lang.txt` use the World-style `o` header prefix, which the importer supports; `notes/Colored/ap-lang.txt` uses the exact World marker format. Original and colored downloads are named `ap-lang.txt` and `ap-lang-colored.txt` and match their current source files exactly. AP Lang uses the approved palette markers `g` (dark green, `#168438`), `li` (lime green, `#79c900`), `pu` (purple, `#883ac2`), `br` (brown, `#92552f`), `y` (medium blue, `#287db3`), `p` (pink, `#ed8eb4`), `lb` (light blue, `#59b8e7`), `hp` (hot pink, `#ec228c`), and `pen` (neutral): pencil body text with selective keyword highlights in one or two colors per line, neutral prefixes and punctuation, designated reserved non-`lb` section-title colors, and light-blue topic/page headings. The colored sources minimally remap `(r)` to `(hp)` and `(db)` to `(y)`; source plaintext, wording, order, and highlight boundaries are unchanged. The worked connection is explicitly hypothetical, not a quotation from the article; other examples are paraphrases. They illustrate plausible, text-grounded audience responses, not guaranteed reactions or proof that labels alone caused reported outcomes.

The **Algebra 2 Notes** link opens `algebra-2.html`, titled **Algebra 2: Functions and Linear Systems**. Thirteen lessons across Modules 1B and 2A cover linear equations and inequalities, piecewise functions, step functions, absolute value, parent functions, translations, dilations, combined transformations, equations of linear functions, solving systems of equations graphically and algebraically, systems of inequalities, and optimization with linear programming. Each lesson has 58 source lines in six sections of 10, 10, 9, 10, 10, and 9 lines, split into two 29-line printable pages: twenty-six physical pages and 754 lines total. The five Module 2A lessons are original study summaries based on the McGraw Hill Module 2A (2.3-2.7) classroom slides, PDF pp. 1-182; examples are practice, not an official answer key, and the PDF is not bundled. These are study-guide notes for the packet, not fabricated reconstructions of unavailable graphs; invented examples are identified, and known worked-answer errors are corrected before publishing. Sources are `notes/algebra-2/<file>.txt` and `notes/algebra-2/Colored/<file>.txt`. Original and colored downloads are named `<file>.txt` and `<file>-colored.txt` and preserve the respective source bytes exactly. Notes use the existing inks and marker style: `o` headers, `->` support lines, initial `(pen)`, neutral prefixes, punctuation, formulas and numbers, selective letter/space highlights with immediate pencil resets and at most two non-pencil colors per line, and uniform non-`lb` section-title ink. Stripped text is ASCII and at most 65 characters per line.

The **Of Mice And Men Analysis** link opens `of-mice-and-men.html`, titled **Of Mice and Men**, a complete companion to John Steinbeck's 1937 novella built around the Active Reading Pamphlet. Its twelve sections - Home, Author, Context, Characters, Plot, Setting, Narration, Attitude, Vocabulary, Theme, Questions, and Works Cited - cover every pamphlet field: the four character qualities (mental, moral, physical, social) for George, Lennie, Candy, Crooks, Curley, Curley's wife, Slim, and the boss; the four setting elements; narration traits with influence on the reader; attitude-versus-mood with the handout's vocabulary and emotion wheel; the Big Ideas and theme-statement template; and prompt questions with example responses. The Home section maps each pamphlet field to its section. Novel scenes and quotations use chapter and short search-phrase locators; readers should record page numbers from their own edition. Short quotations never reproduce the novella's slur. The page shares Home's academic shell, the accessibility controls, and the analysis page pattern of optional JavaScript section navigation with an offline `file://` fallback. Its book cover is a local image; it needs no remote resources.

The **Out Of My Mind Analysis** link opens `out-of-my-mind.html`, now organized on the same twelve-section spine: Home, Author, Context, Characters, Plot, Setting, Point of View, Tone, Vocabulary, Theme, Questions, and Works Cited. Its Home map connects every pamphlet field to the relevant section, including vocabulary, theme/Big Ideas, and questions; character entries pair a key scene with all four character qualities. The companion cites all seven supplied handouts by exact PDF filename. Those PDFs are privately supplied local Active Reading Pamphlet course materials; they are provenance for the field requirements, not files bundled into the site. Novel evidence uses scene descriptions and search locators rather than edition-specific page numbers, since pagination varies; readers should record page numbers from their own copy. Model interpretations and suggested reactions are prompts, not a substitute for each reader's own evidence, interpretation, or personal response. Like its companion, the page retains the shared shell and works as a complete document without JavaScript.

## Build and Import

The generator needs Node.js 20 or later; the existing pinned validation tools need Node.js 22.16 or later (Node 24 is recommended). Use npm. All dependency and package versions are unchanged.

```sh
npm ci
npx playwright install chromium
npm run build
npm test
```

The AP-subject loader imports original and marked `.txt` files byte-for-byte; it does not author or recolor those notes. `.gitattributes` enforces LF checkouts so embedded downloads preserve source bytes. Separately, the FBLA compiler validates structured factual modules and generates original plus color-only companion files. It checks exact stripped equality, the 48-line/two-page format and current local manifest. Initial AP import after independent source review:

```sh
node scripts/build.mjs --unit1 "../source-notes/unit-1" --unit2 "../source-notes/unit-2" --unit3 "../source-notes/unit-3" --unit4 "../source-notes/unit-4" --preview "./preview/preview.html"
```

Rebuild with `npm run build`. The default build regenerates Home, the three subject note pages, Algebra flashcards, the FBLA hub and four event courses. The literary companions remain separately maintained and are copied. Previews include all thirteen HTML pages, thirteen JPEGs, shared styles, note sources, the FBLA pilot, all 88 course encodings, and the course/source manifests. The requested custom preview filename still opens World notes; sibling `index.html` opens Home. Keep the complete export together. Export using:

```sh
node scripts/build.mjs --preview "./preview/preview.html"
```

Preview export copies `notes/unit-1` through `notes/unit-4` beside the exported HTML. Each topic provides two kinds of native links: `Download` original/marked links embed exact source bytes as base64 `text/plain` data URLs for one-click downloads with JavaScript disabled over both HTTP and `file://`; `Open original file` and `Open colored file` links use relative paths to `notes/unit-N/file.txt` and `notes/unit-N/Colored/file.txt`. Relative files and embedded downloads preserve the same source bytes; LF line endings are enforced at checkout by `.gitattributes`, not rewritten by the generator. Chromium opens these relative local text links instead of forcing a download. The 26 official-topic originals plus two separate Unit 3 supplements, and their `Colored` counterparts, are imported into four unit directories. Standalone source previews, authoring validators, and private metadata are not imported. Original downloads retain `<file>.txt`; existing Unit 1 and 2 download names are unchanged. Colored Unit 3 and Unit 4 downloads use `-colored.txt`. All HTML text is escaped before rendering.

## Presentation

All three literary companions use identical twelve-section headings, section containers, and a title/author/cover/introduction/action hero. The cover sits alongside the title on larger screens and uses the same compact mobile layout for each book. Novel vocabulary uses `dl.definition-list` with term/definition pairs; book-specific supplementary handout material remains separate. Shared pamphlet-map, setting, vocabulary, and theme subsection labels are consistent without requiring identical book-specific analysis. `tests/literature.spec.mjs` checks these shared structures, intrinsic cover dimensions, and undistorted responsive covers at 375px, 760px, and 1280px in LTR and forced RTL.

`styles/site.css` supplies the academic shell: paper `#f6f3eb`, ink `#213749`, Georgia body text, responsive wrapper, masthead, navigation, visible focus and footer. Each page links it once via `styles/site.css`. Existing literary controls and subject content are preserved. Approved inks and light-blue topic headings retain the documented contrast exception, with original plaintext alternatives. World History prints 56 pages and AP Lang four, at 24 notes per page; Algebra 2 prints 26 at 29 notes per page. The four FBLA courses print 28/26/24/10 pages with 24 numbered lines each. Their hub and retained pilot are flowing references outside that sheet count.

All numbered course pages use the shared `.notes-site` presentation in `styles/site.css`: warm-white `#fffefb` paper, a fine border and ruled note rows, Georgia note text, small 9px system-font line numbers, shared captions, and logical gutter spacing. FBLA pages reuse the same `.pages` / `.sheet` markup rather than maintaining a separate imitation. The two-column sheet view stacks below 960px, with smaller padding and 13px notes below 480px. The named `notes` A4 print page and row-height variable are scoped to notes pages so the literary companions and Home do not inherit course pagination. Algebra retains its denser row height; all other numbered courses retain 24-line sheets.

The neutral paper and Georgia typography follow the approved Unit 2 preview. The legacy palette remains unchanged for Units 1–3, AP Lang, and Algebra: `y` renders medium blue (`#287db3`), the shared palette has nine highlight inks, and legacy `(db)` remains unsupported; `(r)` remapping to `(hp)` is unchanged. Unit 4 explicitly opts into a separate eleven-ink palette: `g`, `li`, `pu`, `or`, `y` (`#ebc600`), `p`, `lb`, `hp`, `r` (`#df3034`), `db` (`#173f88`), and `mb` (`#287db3`), plus neutral pencil. This scoped override does not redefine legacy `y` or `db`, and does not imply that all subjects use the Unit 4 palette. Topic headings retain light blue (`#59b8e7`); complete marked section titles have uniform colors in headings and Overview listings. On small screens pages stack; all topics remain visible. Printing uses A4 portrait with one 24-line note page per physical page (56 pages), repeating topic titles on each page. Browser print headers/footers should be off and background graphics on for the closest match.

AP Lang and Algebra 2 inherit the shared World print font, but line rows are subject-specific. AP Lang keeps the 8.8mm row height and prints 24 notes per physical page, as does AP World History with 56 pages. Algebra 2 uses a denser 8mm row height so 29 lines fit on an A4 sheet, across twenty-six pages.

## Accessibility Limitation

Semantic landmarks/headings, native keyboard-accessible links, a skip link, visible focus, and a complete no-JavaScript document are provided. AP World History retains the legacy palette in Units 1–3 and the explicitly requested eleven-ink palette in Unit 4; AP Lang retains the legacy palette. Several pastel inks, Unit 4's yellow and light-blue topic/page headings fail WCAG text contrast requirements. This is a documented approved-palette contrast exception, not a WCAG compliance claim. Axe still runs its contrast rule; both subject pages permit only `color-contrast` violations on approved ink, topic-title, and page-title elements and reject every other violation. Original pencil-only text downloads are available as an alternative for every AP World History topic and AP Lang. Manual assistive-technology and visual review remains advisable.

Algebra 2 shares the same approved-palette contrast limitation, not a WCAG compliance claim. Each lesson also offers an original pencil-only text download as an alternative.
